2. Order Flow & Liquidity Tools

Overview

Understanding where major buyers and sellers are active can significantly improve trading performance. Order Flow & Liquidity Tools provide visibility into market activity that is often hidden from traditional charting methods.

Key Capabilities
-Liquidity heatmaps
-Market depth visualization
-Footprint charts
-Volume delta analysis
-Order flow monitoring
-Institutional activity tracking

How It Works

The platform visualizes areas where significant liquidity exists. Traders can quickly identify zones where large market participants may enter or exit positions.

Benefits

-Better trade timing
-Improved entry and exit decisions
-Increased market awareness
-Greater understanding of institutional behavior

User Value

Users gain access to information typically available only on professional trading platforms.
